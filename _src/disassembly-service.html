Method

How we dismantle

Flatpack furniture only survives a move if it comes apart in the same order it went together. Every item follows the same three lines.

  • Fittings in a labelled bag, taped to the item

    Screws, cam locks, dowels and hinges go into a bag marked with the item and the panel. The bag travels with the furniture, not in a loose box.

  • Panels stacked and protected

    Stacked in assembly order, finished faces together, so nothing scratches on the truck and the first panel you need is on top.

  • Optional re-assembly at the new place

    Say so in the notes of the quote form, with the new suburb and postcode, and we quote it for you.

Troubleshooting

Questions people ask before a move

Can you re-assemble it at the new place?

Yes, if you want it. Tell us in the notes of the quote form, with the new suburb and postcode. Because every bag is labelled per item and the panels are stacked in order, the rebuild goes straight from the top of the stack.

Do you take the furniture away?

No. This is dismantling only. We take the item apart, bag the fittings and stack the panels ready to be carried. Your removalist moves it, or you do. If you want the furniture gone rather than moved, that’s disposal, and it isn’t part of this service.

How should I prepare?

Empty the drawers and shelves, take everything off the top, and clear the space around the item so we can lay the panels flat. If the item is anchored to the wall, leave the bracket in place — we remove it and bag the screws with the rest. Let us know about stairs, lifts or narrow doorways in the notes.
